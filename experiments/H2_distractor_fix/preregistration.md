# H2: pre-registration — H with pattern-proof distractors

**Registered:** 2026-10-12T15:56:50Z (UTC). Committed before any H2 item is generated or any model is run. Inherits H
([`../G_scale_7b/preregistration.md`](../G_scale_7b/preregistration.md), `f3ce9d4`) and F (`5f12575`) for model, prompt
layout, probes, CV, seeds, baselines, permutation correction, figures and decision rules. **Only the distractor
construction changes**, plus the pattern-only prerequisite and the scenario definitions below.

## Why
H's distractors (correct ± a subset of {1, 2, 10}) let the correct option be identified from the five option values alone
(200/200 items). H's probes, especially at the option-E token, may have read that pattern rather than a computed product.

## Items (task `C2`)
- Correct: c = a × b with a, b ~ U{10..20} (independent).
- **Distractors:** draw (a′, b′) ~ U{10..20}² independently and keep a′ × b′ if it differs from c and from distractors already
  kept, until there are 4. Distractors therefore follow the **same product distribution as c** (frequency of a product can't
  identify it).
- Shuffle the 5 values uniformly into (A)–(E). Item RNG: `random.Random(201)`; exemplar RNG: `random.Random(1201)`
  (3 exemplars with correct letters forced to B, A, C as in H; their questions are excluded from the item stream).
- Prompts, CoT worked steps, answer-only format, raw text, stop-at-`\nQ:`, and parsing are identical to H variant C.

## Step 0 (prerequisite): pattern-only baseline — commit before any model run
- Data: **5,000** items generated by the H2 rule (seed 9201) and, as a positive control, 5,000 by H's original rule (seed 9101).
  No model, no activations, no question text.
- Per-option features: value, rank among the 5, the 4 sorted absolute differences to the other options, mean absolute difference,
  and an is-median flag. Two scorers trained to predict "is the correct option" with 5-fold CV over items; prediction = argmax over the
  5 options: (i) logistic regression (standardized), (ii) random forest (300 trees, seed 0). Also the rules "median value" and
  "centre-of-offsets" (the unique option whose differences to all others are in {±1, ±2, ±10}; if none or several, random among the tied).
- **Gate:** the best of (i), (ii) and the two rules on **H2 items ≤ 0.30** accuracy (chance 0.20). If > 0.30, **stop and redesign**
  (report back; nothing further runs). Positive control expectation: on H's items the best scorer is ≈ 1.0. If it isn't, the detector is
  considered too weak and that's reported, but the gate decision stays on the H2 number.

## Step 1: pilot (same as H)
30 items (the first 30 of the item stream): no-CoT ∈ [0.55, 0.85], top no-CoT letter ≤ 60%, with-CoT ≥ 0.85. If any fail, stop and report.
That explicitly includes no-CoT > 0.85: wide product spread may make magnitude estimation sufficient. There's no redesign in that case.

## Step 2: full run (same as H)
N = 200, layers 0–27, Target A (no-CoT letter) and Target B (with-CoT letter), 5 seeds, chance / majority / random-Gaussian
baselines, max-over-layers permutation (100), and the four positional checks. Shifted token = question end − 5, which is expected to be the
"(E" token again (all products are 3-digit). The tokenization is verified and reported.

## Decision rules (unchanged from H/F)
- **Target A supported** iff acc@peak > majority_A + 0.05 and the CI lower bound > majority_A.
- **Target B supported** iff acc@peak > chance + 0.10 and the CI lower bound > chance.
- **Positional bias ruled out** iff the last-token probe beats both position-only and shifted-token by ≥ 0.10 with non-overlapping CIs.
- **Inconclusive** if a rule passes uncorrected but the max-over-layers permutation p ≥ 0.05.

## Verdict scenarios (operationalized)
Let "E-token better" mean shifted-token acc − last-token acc ≥ 0.10 **and** non-overlapping CIs (at that target's peak layer).
1. **Replicates:** both targets supported at the last token, and E-token is **not** better for either target.
2. **Pattern artifact:** neither target supported at the last token, and the E-token probe also fails the same rule.
3. **Option-position story:** a target is *not* supported at the last token but *is* at the E-token (rule applied to E-token accuracy),
   or both are supported and E-token is better for at least one target.
4. **Mixed:** anything else, described explicitly.
